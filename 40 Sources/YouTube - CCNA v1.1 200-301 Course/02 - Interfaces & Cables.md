- Ethernet is a collection of network protocols/standards.
- 1 Kb = 1,000 bits
- 1 Mb = 1,000,000 bits
- 1 Gb = 1,000,000,000 bits
- 1 Tb = 1,000,000,000,000 bits
- Ethernet Standards is defined in the IEEE 802.3 standard in 1983
![[Pasted image 20260831135840.png]]
> BASE = baseband signaling
> T = twisted pair
- The copper cables used in Ethernet standards are UTP (Unshielded Twisted (Protects against EMI) Pair) cables
- 2 pairs (4 wires): 10BASE-T, 100BASE-T
- 4 pairs (8 wires): 1000BASE-T, 10GBASE-T
